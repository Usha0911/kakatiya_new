Useful to know

Your questions, considered.

Which technologies can you work with?

Our published services include HTML, PHP, ASP.NET, WordPress, Joomla and Drupal. Technology selection depends on your requirements and the support needs of the project.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We begin by reviewing the content, URLs and useful functionality already in place, then agree what to retain, improve or retire.

How long will a website take?

The schedule depends on scope, integrations, content and review time. Your proposal sets milestones after discovery; we do not quote a universal delivery window.
