Terms for using this informational website. Updated 12 October 2026.

About this website

This site describes the services and historical work of Kakatiya Solutions. It provides general information and a way to begin a project conversation. It is not a binding proposal or a promise of a particular project outcome.

Project enquiries and quotations

Submitting or preparing an enquiry does not create a service agreement. Scope, fees, taxes, schedules, third-party costs, revisions, intellectual property and support responsibilities must be agreed in a separate written proposal or contract.

Budget ranges in the quote form help start a conversation. They are not fixed prices. No ranking, lead volume, revenue increase, delivery time or support availability is guaranteed by this website.

Historical portfolio material

Portfolio names, images and client comments are drawn from the company’s published archive. They may describe historical relationships and earlier website versions. They do not imply an ongoing retainer, a current endorsement or ownership of a client’s brand.

Where measured outcomes or detailed project records are unavailable, the site identifies that limitation. Initials used beside client feedback are visual labels, not portraits.

Intellectual property

Company and client names, logos and portfolio materials remain subject to their respective owners’ rights. Do not reuse those materials as your own work. Third-party fonts and software retain their original licences.

Responsible use

Provide accurate contact information for your own enquiry. Do not submit spam, unlawful material, credentials or confidential information through the forms. Do not interfere with the website or attempt unauthorised access to its hosting systems.

External services and availability

Third-party links, map content, fonts and other external resources may change or become unavailable. Enquiry delivery through your email application depends on your own email service. The website does not report an email as delivered merely because it has prepared a draft.

Questions and corrections

If you identify an inaccurate portfolio attribution or need clarification, contact info@kakatiyasolutions.com. Specific project terms should be settled in the written engagement agreement.
