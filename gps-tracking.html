Useful to know

Your questions, considered.

Does tracking work everywhere?

Location reporting depends on device capability, GPS reception, mobile connectivity and the selected service. Coverage must be checked for your operating areas.

Can we use devices we already own?

Compatibility depends on device protocols and the platform. Share device models and integration documentation for review.

Is hardware included?

Hardware, installation, connectivity and software charges are separate scope items to confirm in the proposal.
