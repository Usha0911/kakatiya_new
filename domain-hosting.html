Useful to know

Your questions, considered.

What does hosting cost?

Pricing depends on the plan, application and support scope. Historical promotional prices on the old site are not current quotations.

Is email affected by a website move?

It can be if DNS or mail records change. We identify email dependencies before any migration.

Are backups and support included?

Backup frequency, restoration responsibilities and support hours must be specified in your chosen plan.
