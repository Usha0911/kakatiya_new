How this website handles enquiries, browser preferences and external services. Updated 12 October 2026.

Who to contact

This website presents Kakatiya Solutions in Hanamkonda, Warangal, Telangana, India. For a question about information you share with us, email info@kakatiyasolutions.com or call +91 939 080 7611.

Information you choose to provide

Enquiry forms ask for your name, email, optional organisation and phone number, project details, and your permission to respond. The quote form also asks for a service, budget range and timeline. Do not include passwords, payment card information or other confidential material.

How forms work in this version

By default, the form prepares an email link in your browser. The website does not automatically send or store that enquiry. If you choose to open the link, your email application handles the draft; you decide whether to send it. A newsletter request works the same way and does not automatically add you to a mailing list.

If a hosted form service is enabled in a later deployment, this policy must be updated to identify that provider and explain the delivery and retention arrangements before the change goes live.

Responding to enquiries

Details you send by email are used to understand and respond to your request. You can ask for correction or deletion of enquiry information by contacting us. Any project-related recordkeeping and retention arrangements should be agreed during the engagement.

Browser storage and optional maps

The site stores a small preference named ks-consent in your browser to remember your map choice. You can change it using Cookie preferences in the footer or clear it through your browser settings. The website includes no analytics or advertising tracker.

Google Maps is loaded only when you allow maps or press the map’s load button. Once loaded, Google receives a network request and may process information under its own policies. Revoking permission removes the embedded map but cannot reverse information already sent.

Fonts, libraries and hosting

Fonts are delivered by Google Fonts and interface libraries by jsDelivr. Your browser contacts these services to retrieve the assets, which exposes ordinary request information such as an IP address. These connections occur when the page loads. The hosting provider may also maintain ordinary access logs.

External links

Social profiles, WhatsApp and external map links take you to third-party services. Their information handling is governed by their own policies. No social widgets are embedded in this site.

Changes

Review this page when the website’s forms, hosting or external services change. The date above identifies this version.
